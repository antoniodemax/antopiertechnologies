# Antopier Technologies — Agency-First Roadmap

**Planning window:** 90 days, 12 November 2026 – 9 February 2027  
**Status:** Proposed. CEO approval required at each gate.  
**Business model:** Technology consultancy / digital solutions agency first; product development follows validated customer demand.

## North star
Build a credible, revenue-generating technology consultancy. Deliver valuable client work, standardise the strongest services, and use recurring customer problems to decide whether Antopier Insights should become a product.

## Phase 0 — Baseline and business clarity (Days 1–7)
**Outcome:** Know what exists and what Antopier can credibly sell now.
- Audit repository, live website content, technical stack, forms and deployment.
- Preserve the existing website and its consultancy positioning.
- Review actual founder capabilities, portfolio evidence and delivery capacity.
- Define a truthful service catalogue and exclusions.
- Create proposal, discovery, scope, delivery and handover templates.
- Inspect installed Claude Code skills, including exact Matt Pocock and Fluent AI skill names.
**Gate:** CEO approves the service positioning, offer shortlist and any website copy changes.

## Phase 1 — Agency offer and customer discovery (Days 8–21)
**Outcome:** Choose one or two services to sell first and identify reachable buyers.
- Compare web/application development, BI dashboards/reporting, AI integration and consulting by access, ability to deliver, urgency and likely economics.
- Prepare a discovery interview guide and prospect qualification criteria.
- Conduct conversations; record only actual evidence.
- Identify a first target segment and buyer.
- Draft a simple service offer with deliverables, exclusions, timeline assumptions and acceptance criteria.
- Create a lightweight lead/proposal pipeline.
**Gate:** CEO approves the first target segment and initial paid offer. No automated outreach or customer contact by agents without permission.

## Phase 2 — Service delivery system (Days 22–35)
**Outcome:** Be ready to deliver a small paid engagement professionally.
- Standardise discovery, requirements, scope, pricing estimation, change control, QA, handover and support.
- Build a synthetic BI demonstration only if BI is selected as an initial offer.
- Define data handling rules and client confidentiality.
- Draft statement-of-work and proposal templates for professional review.
- Create a simple case-study process requiring client permission.
**Gate:** CEO approves the service package, pricing approach, client contract process and delivery checklist.

## Phase 3 — First paid work and learning (Days 36–49)
**Outcome:** Pursue and deliver scoped work; capture evidence.
- CEO-led outreach and discovery.
- Prepare proposals only for qualified opportunities.
- Agree written scope, payment milestones, data access and support before delivery.
- Deliver approved work with QA and acceptance.
- Track actual effort, cost, scope changes, customer value and margin.
**Gate:** Review actual demand, delivery economics and customer feedback. If no paid work is secured, revisit the offer rather than assuming demand.

## Phase 4 — Standardise repeatable services (Days 50–63)
**Outcome:** Improve repeatability and quality.
- Refine reusable templates, technical patterns and quality gates.
- Identify service retainers or recurring reporting only where clients value them.
- Assess which problems recur across independent customers.
- If BI is repeated, document common data formats, metric definitions and workflow friction.
**Gate:** CEO decides whether Antopier Insights merits a formal product discovery phase.

## Phase 5 — Antopier Insights product discovery (Days 64–76)
**Outcome:** Convert evidence into a narrow product definition, not code.
- Interview users about repeated spreadsheet reporting pain.
- Compare manual service, reusable dashboard templates and SaaS.
- Define a narrow customer segment, workflow, metric dictionary and success measures.
- Draft product PRD, synthetic dataset specification and user journey.
- Compare architecture options based on current repo: separate product repo, isolated workspace/app, or other boundary.
- Review Supabase/PostgreSQL security, tenant isolation, privacy and cost.
**Gate:** CEO approves or rejects product build. A product may be deferred if a repeatable paid problem is not demonstrated.

## Phase 6 — Technical design and implementation decision (Days 77–84)
**Outcome:** If evidence supports a product, prepare a secure and affordable design.
- Complete architecture decision record and threat model.
- Define database schema, RLS, private storage, upload validation and AI data flow.
- Define test strategy, backup/restore, monitoring and cost envelope.
- Break approved scope into small implementation tasks.
**Gate:** CEO approves a specific implementation task, budget and environment. No blanket approval.

## Phase 7 — Review and next-quarter strategy (Days 85–90)
**Outcome:** Make decisions from evidence.
- Review revenue, pipeline, conversion, delivery margin and repeat work.
- Review customer problems and service demand.
- Review whether Antopier Insights is validated enough to build.
- Assess risks, budget and capacity.
- Produce next-quarter roadmap with priorities and owners.
**Gate:** CEO chooses continue, pivot, pause or invest.

## Stop/go rules
- Do not build SaaS because it sounds scalable; validate repeated demand and willingness to pay.
- Do not promise services beyond proven capability or available capacity.
- Do not use client data without explicit permission and appropriate controls.
- Security/privacy issues block production data processing.
- Any spend beyond the approved budget requires CEO approval.
- The website is not rewritten just to make the company look like a product startup.
